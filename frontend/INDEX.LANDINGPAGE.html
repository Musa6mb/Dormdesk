About DormDesk

DormDesk is a smart student accommodation management platform designed to simplify maintenance reporting and communication between students and residence administrators.


Students can report maintenance issues, track request progress, and receive updates while administrators manage housing operations more efficiently.

Help & FAQ

+ What is DormDesk?
DormDesk is a platform that helps students report and monitor accommodation issues.
+ How do I report an issue?
Login as a student and submit a maintenance request from your dashboard.
+ Who can use DormDesk?
Students, administrators and residence staff.
